## Observations
- **The pipeline works on GitHub end to end**: data rebuilt from the pinned revision, index
  built, the ratchet accepted the re-stamped baseline as metadata-only, retrieval matched
  the local baseline within its known embedding noise (2 lost, 1 gained of 200).
- **The FAIL is a false alarm.** Nothing in PR #2 produces answers; the rise is a fresh draw
  against the baseline's draw. The five fresh golden runs on record put unsupported-answer
  rate at 0.5714–0.6711 — the baseline is the lowest of them — and the largest gap between
  any two is 0.0997, against a threshold of 0.064 (MIS-047).
- **65 golden answers changed judged outcome** — every answer was regenerated.
- **The judge failed on 2 answers, and judging took 25 minutes against ~5 locally.** Slow
  responses reaching the 120 s timeout is the likely cause; not confirmed, because the
  artifact records that the judge failed, not why.
- **GitHub's cache now holds GitHub's answers**, not the baseline's: a later unchanged PR
  would replay 0.64 against 0.5714 and fail the same way (MIS-048).

## Anomalies
- Judge failures on GitHub (2 of 77), never seen in five local fresh runs (0 of ~385).
- Judging ~5x slower on GitHub than locally.
