## Question

Does cutting where the topic changes — rather than every 600 words — change
document-level retrieval on this corpus?

## Configuration diff

```yaml

   chunk_size: 600                   threshold_percentile: 95

```

## Observations

Read from `rag diff` on `dev`, all 21 flipped questions, with how many chunks of the
gold article reach the 50-chunk pool.

- **The 95th-percentile rule cut far more than expected.** 19,296 chunks from 6,221
  articles — three per article, and **only 2.9% of articles stayed whole** against the
  control's 79.0%. Mean chunk length 123 words. So on this corpus "cut at topic
  changes" is not a gentler version of fixed chunking; it is a much finer one, because
  the rule places a boundary at the largest distance *within each article* regardless
  of whether that distance is large in absolute terms. A short, coherent help article
  still has a maximum, so it still gets cut. **H-016 wrong** on the chunk count
  (predicted 9,000–14,000).
- **21 of 200 outcomes changed, net −9, and the paired test cannot separate it from
  the control** (p = 0.078), though it is well outside the 2-question re-embedding
  floor (EXP-0023). Read together: real churn, no reliable direction.
- **Losses look like evidence dilution.** 11 of the 15 losses moved the gold from rank
  1–5 to 6–10; four fell far (*"How to make the published changes draft?"* 5 → 24;
  *"delete a picture off the contact page"* 1 → 29; *"a button that … will reveal info"*
  5 → 33; *"allow users to enter the amount they want to pay"* had one gold go 5 → 53).
  Several lost golds put 2–7 of their chunks in the pool (*"remove the ribbon from one
  plan"*: 7 and 4 chunks for its two golds, ranks 1,3 → 1,7) — the article is
  well represented but its single best 123-word piece scores below other articles'
  best pieces. Max pooling rewards one strong passage, and cutting an article into
  three pieces makes each piece weaker.
- **Gains are the same boundary set as every other Axis 1 config** (*"grid gallery"*
  9 → 1, *"header background … scrolling"* 8 → 5, *"cancel my Premium subscription
  within the 14-day period"* 6 → 2) — the F9/F10 questions whose gold sits at dense
  rank 6–9 and moves under any change.
- **Collapse rose to 1.238** (control 1.106), as with parent-document: finer chunks put
  more pieces of one article near each other. No question exhausted the pool.
- **Procedure blocks: 3,482 of 5,936 cut**, against 0 under the control. The semantic
  boundary does not respect the "To do X:" / steps structure at all — a topic-change
  boundary frequently falls between the header and its steps, because that is a real
  change of wording. Tier 1 cannot see what that costs an answer; a Tier 2 run could.
- **Cost of the technique:** it is the only chunker in the axis that pays twice —
  $0.0080 for the sentence pass (plus ~$0.02 lost to MIS-024) on top of $0.031 for the
  index, and 30 minutes of the boundary pass before a single chunk existed.

## Anomalies

  doing its job.
- `dev` p95 7,097 ms (provider latency; the index was cached and BM25 is not involved).
