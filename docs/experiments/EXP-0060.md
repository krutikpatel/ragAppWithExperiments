## Observations
- **Determinism when nothing changes** — every metric and every per-question outcome equal;
  0 questions changed on `dev`, 0 golden answers changed. The gate cannot false-alarm on an
  unchanged pipeline, because nothing is re-sampled.
- **Run 1's numbers match the measured history**: recall@5 0.72 on dev (EXP-0005) and 0.525
  on the golden slice (P3-03). Its false-answer rate (0.40) is above all three P3-07 runs
  (0.27–0.33); DEC-085 notes it.
- **Two things the first real runs exposed, both fixed and recorded:** replayed answers
  were priced as bought (MIS-044), and `promoted` began resolving to ci-eval's replayed runs;
  and a second answer quoted a URL from its own context, which led to the P1-05 test
  checking provenance instead of shape (DEC-086).
- Wall clock: run 1 ≈ 20 minutes (all fresh); runs 2 and 3 ≈ 5 seconds of model-free work
  after loading.
