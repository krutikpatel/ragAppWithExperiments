## Question

Every chunking experiment rebuilds the index, and EXP-0012 measured that only 59% of
vectors come back byte-identical when the same text is embedded again on DeepInfra
(OQ-023 / OQ-031). How much of an Axis 1 delta is that, and not the chunking?

## Configuration diff

None that determines a vector. `index_dir` only decides where the cache is written,
and it is not part of the index key — which is exactly why this run had to name a
directory by hand rather than change a setting.

## Observations

- **The floor is 2 questions, and it is one-sided here.** Re-embedding the identical
  corpus lost two questions and gained none, both sitting exactly on the rank-5
  boundary. nDCG@10 is unchanged to four decimals, so the ranking as a whole did not
  move — only two documents crossed the cut-off that strict recall@5 reads.
- **It does not explain the Axis 1 results.** Against this floor: sentence-window
  −0.190 (58 flips) and structure −0.055 (23 flips) are outside it; semantic −0.045
  (21 flips) and parent-document −0.025 (29 flips) have many more flips than 2 but
  net deltas within about four times the floor, which is why both are reported as
  "no measurable difference" by the paired test rather than as losses.
- **It corrects a caution in EXP-0022.** That writeup noted 8 of 17 lost questions had
  gold articles that are a single chunk under both chunkers, and said part of the
  −0.055 might be re-embedding. Measured, re-embedding moves 2 questions, so those 8
  are mostly *not* noise: the remaining difference for single-chunk articles is the
  line breaks the structure chunker preserves where the control joins with spaces
  (only 29 of 4,915 single-chunk texts are byte-identical between the two). The
  correction is appended to EXP-0022.
- **This is one rebuild, not a distribution.** A second rebuild would give a spread
  rather than a single count; one run establishes the order of magnitude (single
  digits of questions, not tens), which is what the axis needed.
