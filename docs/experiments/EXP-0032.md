## Question
Does embedding a *generated answer* instead of the question improve retrieval
(Gao et al. 2022)?

## Configuration diff
```yaml
+ query_transform: hyde
+ query_transform_params: {model: openai/gpt-oss-20b, max_words: 120}
```
One query per question — the hypothetical passage **replaces** the question, so there
is no fusion and the dense ranking passes through with its own scores. Zero fallbacks,
zero failed calls, 200/200 passages generated.

## Observations

**The largest multi-document gain any technique in this project has produced, and it is
not significant.** `gold_docs:multi` went 0.350 → 0.450, seven questions gained against
three lost. At n=40 the CI is [−0.050, +0.250] and p = 0.346, so this is **not a
finding** — it is the only positive signal on that slice after MMR, three rerankers,
contextual retrieval and decomposition all failed to move it, and it is worth saying
that plainly rather than burying it. What would settle it is the same technique on
`dev_large`, where the multi-document slice has zero rows (MIS-021), so it cannot be
settled there. Recorded as OQ-045.

**It is the highest-churn technique in the axis.** 43 of 200 questions changed outcome
(20 lost, 23 gained) against decomposition's 19 and multi-query's 24 — and it nets to
+0.015. Replacing a 20-word question with a 120-word generated passage changes the query
vector completely; it changes the answer for a fifth of the split and improves nothing
measurable.

**Single-document recall did not move, as predicted, and the ceiling argument holds.**
0.8125 → 0.8063 with p = 1.000. The story expected HyDE to help single-doc more than
multi-hop; it did the reverse, and the reason single-doc cannot show much is that it
starts at 0.8125 against a measured candidate-depth-50 ceiling of 0.985.

## Hypothesis
H-024: overall within ±0.03 — **correct** (+0.015). The predicted mechanism ("the query
vector is dominated by generic help-article phrasing, so HyDE may be
*anti*-discriminating on a single-product corpus") is **not supported**: if it were,
recall would have fallen, and instead it rose slightly and the multi-doc slice rose the
most of anything measured here. The ceiling half of the hypothesis — that single-doc
cannot show much — held exactly.

## Decision
**Control retained; not promoted.** +0.015 at p = 0.754.
