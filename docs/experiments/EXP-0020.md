## Question

Does indexing small pieces (so a query matches the specific sentence-group) while handing the generator the surrounding 600 words change document-level retrieval? Note the confound P2-07 names: the DEC-040 walk already takes one chunk per article, so "expand to the parent" is priced partly into the control.

## Observations

Read from `rag diff` on `dev`, all 29 flipped questions.

- **A null with churn.** 29 of 200 outcomes changed, 12 up and 17 down, net −5, p = 0.46. The multi-document slice is 3 / 3. The direction check on `dev_large` is a small significant loss (−0.007), driven by ordinary articles (−0.011) — so the two splits do not agree on a gain, and there is no gain to promote.
- **Collapse rose from 1.11 to 1.32 (p90 1.4 → 1.8)** — the first config in Phase 2 to move it. With four children per parent, several children of one article rank together and the document walk scans them; the 50-chunk pool was never exhausted.
- **Gains are the F10 set plus a few genuine finds.** *"add a full PDF to my portfolio site"* 9 → 1, *"header background to black when scrolling"* 8 → 2, *"grid gallery"* 9 → 1, *"give my login to a website designer"* 33 → 4: the short exact-term questions that a small indexed unit matches better — the same questions BM25 wins (F10). Two multi-document gains: *"restore the selection of projects for my collection"* (20,6 → 4,3) and *"Error 403 while trying to modify"* (22,1 → 2,1).
- **Losses are dense's rank 1–5 gold slipping to 6–9** plus three far drops (*"verify my DNS records"* 4 → 16; *"DNS configuration for email forwarding with Squarespace"* 5 → 23; *"connect elements to my dataset"* 2 → 12). Several lost golds have 3–6 children in the top 50 (*"domain privacy included in the price"* 1 → 8 with 5 children in the pool) — the article is well represented in the pool but its best child sits below five other articles' best children. Smaller indexed units spread an article's evidence across pieces; max pooling keeps only the best piece.
- **Procedure cuts:** 1,237 at the child level, 150 at the parent level the generator sees — a Tier 2 run would be the place to see whether that matters; Tier 1 cannot.
- Contributes to **H-013** (no chunker beats the control): holds here.
