cross-lab judge tell supported from unsupported well enough to gate CI?

## Observations
- **Instrument check first:** all 240 answers are exactly three consecutive sentences of
  their recorded source article (verified against the extractor's own split).
- **All 8 flagged supported pairs, read one by one:**
  - **5 contain text that is not a factual claim** — a mechanical cut picked up FAQ
    questions ("What happens when I import a CSV file to my collection?", "How much does it
    cost to renew my Wix domain?"), an article title glued to the next sentence, lead-ins
    that end in a colon ("To enable search engines to index your site:"), and a UI label
    ("Show me an example"). Ragas turns each into a statement the article never asserts.
    These are artifacts of v2's construction, not judge errors about content.
  - **2 are hypothetical examples** — "For example, you have 50 products with the 'New'
    ribbon. You want to remove the ribbon from 10 of them." and a sample email user name.
    Copied verbatim, still flagged. The strict "any unsupported claim" rule reacts to
    illustrations, which generated answers also write.
  - **1 is a plain miss**: `926d5b0f`, three supported sentences about re-crawling after
    edits, scored 0.0.
- **The unsupported side did not move:** 80/80 flagged, mean faithfulness 0.000 — on v2 the
  foreign sentences are real help-article prose in the same register as the context, a
  harder negative than v1's, and the judge still rejects all of them.
- **Hard negatives:** 77 of 80 flagged. Three near-neighbour extracts passed; by design
  those may be genuinely supported by near-duplicate content, and they are not in the bar.

## Limitation (stated in README and NARRATIVE)
This check shows the judge verifies **literal** support and rejects foreign content. It
does **not** measure how the judge treats paraphrase — which is what generated answers
are — and no human agreement was measured. Precision on borderline answers is unknown.

- None else. No retries, no failures.
