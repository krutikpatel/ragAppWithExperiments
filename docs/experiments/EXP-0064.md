## Observations
- **Every drill behaved as specified, with one redefinition made before running:** drill 1's
  "fail on recall@5" cannot happen in this system — recall@5 is read off the ranking, and
  `top_k` only chooses how many ranked articles reach the generator. The gate caught it through
  gold-in-context, which DEC-095 added for exactly this.
- **Drill 4, the one near the floor, failed clearly.** One article fewer moved gold-in-context by
  0.085 — 17 of 200 questions — at p ≈ 0, because the change only loses questions, never gains
  any. Its judged metrics stayed inside their thresholds (faithfulness −0.023, false-answer
  +0.133): the gate caught it through retrieval, not through the answers.
- **Drill 3's false answers, read against the questions:** answered with the rule removed —
  "Where do I change the checkout currency in Squarespace?" (another platform), "How much does
  it cost?" (no subject), "How did the Wix SEO tools change in the 2025 updates?" (after the KB
  snapshot), "How do I set up local pickup for orders in Shopify?", "What were the Wix service
  outages reported in 2025?". Twelve of fifteen answered; none refused.
- **Drill 2 fell on faithfulness in every stratum** — single-doc 0.896 → 0.799, multi-doc
  0.843 → 0.685, unanswerable 0.918 → 0.700. **Its citation metrics went to 1.000**: an answer that
  cites nothing has no bad citation, so citation validity and integrity read as *better*. The
  gate caught drill 2 through faithfulness; it would not catch a change that only stopped the
  generator citing (OQ-050).
- **Drill 1's judged metrics improved** (unsupported-answer −0.158, citation validity +0.092)
  while its retrieval collapsed: with one article, answers are shorter and cite less, so fewer
  claims and citations can go wrong. A judged metric alone would have passed drill 1.

## Anomalies
- The CI artifact records per-answer flags, not answer text, so drill 2's answers could not be
  read to confirm they stopped citing (OQ-050). Drill 3's examples come from the golden slice's
  question text joined to the artifact's per-answer refusal flags.
