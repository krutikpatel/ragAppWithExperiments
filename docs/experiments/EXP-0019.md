## Question

Does indexing the smallest meaningful unit — one sentence — and handing the generator a
±3-sentence window improve retrieval over fixed 600-word chunks?

## Configuration diff

```yaml
 chunker: fixed_token          ->  chunker: sentence_window
 chunker_params:                   chunker_params:
   chunk_size: 600                   window: 3
   overlap: 100
```

## Observations

Read from `rag diff` on `dev` (58 flips; the ten gains and a sample of the losses read
individually), plus the length of the sentences that actually reached the context.

- **A 12-word index row cannot represent a help article.** The median indexed unit is
  12 words and the median sentence that reached a context was 14 words. A query like
  *"How to make the published changes draft?"* has to match a single short sentence
  better than 196k other short sentences; the gold article fell out of the top 100
  entirely for that question (rank 5 → absent) and for *"prevent an item in my CMS
  collection from being published"* (4 → 70, 0 gold chunks in the pool). 48 of 200
  questions lost, 21 of them falling past rank 10.
- **The document-level walk does not rescue it.** This is the confound P2-07 asked to
  be addressed, measured: collapse rose to 1.314 on `dev` and 1.490 on `dev_large`
  (p90 2.2), so the walk is indeed scanning several sentences per article found — but
  the ranking it walks is worse, and no amount of distinct-document selection recovers
  an article whose best sentence ranks 70th. **H-014 half right**: the collapse ratio
  is the highest of the axis (1.314 on `dev`, 1.490 on `dev_large`) but **below the
  predicted 2.0**, because the pool caps the walk at 50 chunks and the gold article
  often has only one or two sentences in it; multi-document strict recall did fall
  (0.350 → 0.250), as predicted, but within noise on n=40.
- **The ten gains are the F10 exact-term family**, and they are the cleanest
  demonstration of what the technique is for: *"add a full PDF to my portfolio site"*
  9 → 2, *"adjusting section heights"* 9 → 1, *"grid gallery"* 9 → 1, *"header
  background to black when scrolling"* 8 → 3, *"resend an email marketing campaign to a
  specific group"* 15 → 5, and two multi-document questions (*"Error 403 while trying
  to modify"* 22,1 → 5,2; *"business email not directed to my Gmail"* 12,5 → 5,3). When
  the answer is one sentence, indexing one sentence finds it. That is 10 questions
  against 48.
- **Both splits agree, which no other Axis 1 config managed.** Structure and semantic
  disagreed or were null; here `dev_large` (−0.072) and `dev` (−0.190) both say
  negative, and the synthetic split understates the damage — its questions were
  generated from their gold article, so they contain more of the gold's wording than a
  real question does.
- **Every procedure block is cut** at the indexed level (5,936 of 5,936 — a 12-word
  sentence cannot contain a 37-word procedure), and 3,263 are still cut in the ±3
  window. If the technique were to be used here, the window would have to be chosen
  against the procedure-block length distribution, not set to a library default.
- **Engineering cost, measured, not a quality claim:** 2.8 hours and 9,286 calls to
  build, 3.2 GB on disk and in memory for a corpus that fits in 135 MB at the control
  setting — a 24x index for a 19-point loss.
