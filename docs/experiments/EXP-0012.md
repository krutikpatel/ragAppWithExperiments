## Question

All three Axis 2 candidates fixed the same 66 synthetic `feature_request` questions the
control misses (C1, answered from stored runs at zero cost: 66 of 67 common; the control
misses 69 of that type). Is the qwen3 instruct prefix on queries — *"retrieve relevant
passages that answer the query"* — the cause, and is removing it free on real questions?

Pre-registered criteria (config header):
- **C2 — the prefix is the cause:** `feature_request` strict recall@5 on `dev_large`
  rises from 0.966 to **≥ 0.99**, p < 0.05.
- **C3 — it is not a trade:** on `dev`, strict recall@5 vs promoted is not significantly
  negative (CI upper bound ≥ 0).

## Observations

- **Outcome by the pre-registered table: !C2 (by threshold) and !C3.** The prefix
  stays. The instruct prefix is not free on synthetic feature-request labels (it costs
  53 of the 69 such misses) and it is far from free to remove on real questions (six
  points of strict recall@5 and 0.057 of nDCG, with every slice down or flat).
- **The prefix accounts for most, not all, of the `feature_request` gap.** Without it
  the control reaches 0.988 on that slice; the three candidates reached 0.999. The
  remaining 16 misses have some other cause (the next hypothesis in OQ-026: the
  "Request:" title template itself). Read against EXP-0009–0011, roughly 4/5 of every
  candidate's `dev_large` gain on that type was the absence of an answer-oriented query
  instruction, not a better model.
- **What the instruction buys, mechanically:** on ordinary articles it puts the gold
  document first more often (+0.023 at k=1 on `dev_large`, p = 0.0001) and on real
  questions it keeps the gold inside the top five. Lost `dev` questions are terse or
  indirect phrasings whose gold moved from rank 3–5 to 6–15: *"i wish to cancel a
  plan"* (3 → 11), *"a button that people can click and it will reveal info"* (5 → 15,
  gold *Adding and Setting Up Collapsible Text*), *"Why is the UI in the tutorial
  different from mine…"* (4 → 8), *"change DNS configuration… email forwarding with
  Squarespace"* (5 → 12), and the two-gold *"allow users to enter the amount they want
  to pay"* ([5, 1] → [10, 1]). Short questions lost most (−0.122, p = 0.004) — the
  instruction seems to matter most when the query carries the least.
- **The synthetic `feature_request` questions reward *matching the request*, real
  questions reward *answering*.** A "Can I upload XML files?" question's gold is the
  article saying the feature is requested; the control's first pick was the how-to for
  uploading documents. Which of those is the better retrieval for a support assistant
  is not something `dev_large` can adjudicate, and this run does not try to.
- Index-side determinism (OQ-023): a second build of the same passages on DeepInfra
  reproduced 59% of vectors byte-for-byte and the rest to cosine ≥ 0.99977. Two builds
  of one config are one index for every metric here.
