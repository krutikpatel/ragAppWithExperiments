## Observations

- **The splits disagree in direction, and not marginally.** `dev_large` says +0.005
  (p = 0.019); `dev` says −0.160 (p = 0.0001), the largest retrieval delta measured in
  this project after dense-vs-sparse. Under DEC-050 the candidate is refused because
  `dev` does not agree; had `dev_large` been the sole decider it would have promoted a
  model that loses 40 of 200 real questions. This is the direct evidence for DEC-055.
- **The `dev_large` gain is the same `feature_request` effect as EXP-0009** — 67 gained /
  1 lost on that type, versus a *significant loss* on ordinary articles (−0.008,
  p = 0.002). Two very different models fix the same ~67 synthetic questions on
  "Request:" articles, so that gain says something about the control on that article
  type (OQ-026), not about either candidate.
- **On `dev` the losses are everywhere**: every slice is down, short questions most
  (−0.230), simulated questions more than expert-written (−0.210 vs −0.110),
  multi-document nDCG by 0.209. Lost questions are not near-boundary shuffles: gold
  ranks move from 1–5 to 9–48 (*"How to make the published changes draft?"* 5 → 48;
  *"add more static pages"* 1 → 9; *"cancel both site plan and domain"* [1, 2, 4] →
  [1, 13, 5]), and one two-gold question (*"grant a developer access… Beam payment"*,
  gold *Roles & Permissions: Overview*) went from ranks [2, 1] to not in the top 100.
- **Synthetic questions flatter this model.** `dev_large` questions were generated from
  their article and share its wording; `dev` questions are user phrasings (*"make the
  published changes draft"* for an article about saving and publishing). bge-m3, 1024-d
  and trained heavily on multilingual paraphrase, tracks surface wording well enough to
  be near ceiling on the former and 16 points worse on the latter. This is preflight
  item 3 ("never quote a `dev_large` number as a headline") measured rather than
  asserted — and it applies to a dense model, not only to BM25.
- The index is a quarter the control's size (33.7 vs 134.6 MB) at the same per-token
  price ($0.0355 vs $0.0313 per index), but the endpoint's 8,192-token batch limit
  forced 1,644 calls and a 24-minute build (MIS-023).
- Collapse ratio is the lowest of the three models (1.058 mean on `dev_large`, 1.053 on
  `dev`): adjacent chunks of one article cluster less. Exhaustion 0.
