## Question

Does cutting only where the article's own lines end — and never between a "To do X:" header and its steps — change document-level retrieval against fixed 600/100? (Redefined from "on headings": the corpus has none, DEC-057.)

## Observations

Read from `rag diff` on `dev`, all 23 flipped questions, with the number of chunks the gold article has under each chunker.

- **The `dev` loss is mostly boundary slips, and half of it is on articles the chunker did not re-cut.** 14 of the 17 losses moved the gold from rank 2–5 to rank 6–9 (*"How to make the published changes draft?"* 5 → 9; *"change the name of a menu item"* 4 → 7; *"connect elements to my dataset"* 2 → 6); three dropped far (*"how to see my website ip address"* 5 → 29; *"How do I get notified of purchases"* 4 → 37; *"a button that people can click and it will reveal info"* 5 → 49). **8 of the 17 lost questions have a gold article that is a single chunk under both chunkers** — identical words, so no cut moved. What differs for those 4,915 single-chunk articles is that the structure chunker keeps the article's line breaks where the control joins with spaces (only 29 of the 4,915 texts are byte-identical), and that the index is a fresh embedding pass. Neither is "structure-aware splitting"; both are inside the measured −0.055.
- **The re-embedding floor for a chunking comparison has not been measured.** EXP-0012 found 59% of vectors byte-identical when the same text was embedded again; the control's run-to-run spread on `dev` (0.005, three runs) was measured on a *cached* index, so it does not include re-embedding. Every chunking config is a fresh embed. Until a control-rebuild run exists, no chunking delta smaller than that floor can be called (OQ-031, proposed).
- **Six gains are the usual boundary set** (*"cancel my Premium subscription within the 14-day period"* 6 → 2; *"grid gallery"* 9 → 1; *"header background to black when scrolling"* 8 → 5) — three of these six also appeared as gains in Axis 3's fusion runs, i.e. they are questions whose gold sits at dense rank 6–9 and moves on any perturbation (F9).
- **Splits disagree in direction, as in EXP-0010.** `dev_large` +0.0088 (p = 0.0002) is **entirely the `feature_request` slice** (+0.026, 54 gained, 0 lost); ordinary articles are +0.0005 (p = 0.90). This is the same slice EXP-0012 traced to the query prefix. Under DEC-055 the `dev_large` gain cannot promote and the direction check fails.
- **Procedure cuts: 41 vs 0.** The control's 100-word overlap already holds every procedure block whole (corpus profile); the structure chunker, with no overlap, cuts 41 that sit inside single lines over 600 words. So on this corpus the stated benefit of structure-aware cutting — keeping procedures intact — was already delivered by overlap, and the number the story asked for reads: fixed chunking split 0 procedures, line-aware chunking split 41. The multi-document slice moved 0 / 2.
- **H-015 wrong on `dev`** (|Δ| = 0.055, not ≤ 0.02; p = 0.03), right on `dev_large` (+0.009 is outside ±0.005 too — wrong there as well, in the other direction). Resolution in HYPOTHESES.md.
