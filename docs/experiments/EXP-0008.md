## Observations

- **`dev_large` contains no multi-document questions.** `n_gold_docs` is 1 for all
  6,221 rows (counted from the split, not inferred from the metrics). Strict and loose
  recall are therefore identical at every k, and MRR's "single-gold subset" is the
  whole split. The slice the organizing question is about does not exist here. See
  OQ-024 (answered) and MIS-021.
- **The control is near ceiling.** 168 of 6,221 questions miss at k=5 (2.7%); 119 of
  those have the gold document at rank 6–20 and 49 outside the top 20. Any embedding
  candidate can gain at most 2.7 points of strict recall@5 on this split.
- **Sampled misses (6, seed 8) are mostly near-duplicate articles or doubtful gold
  labels, not retrieval failures in the ordinary sense.** Four of six put a title-level
  match at rank 1 while the gold is a sibling article: *Connecting PostFinance* over
  the gold *Connecting Paystack* (gold rank 7); *Converting an Existing Page to a
  Dynamic Page* over the gold *About Dynamic Pages* (rank 7); *Managing Your Resources
  Using the Wix App* over the gold *Request: Managing and Allocating Rooms* (rank 14);
  *Marking a Session as "Paid"* over the gold *Viewing and Editing Client Info* (rank
  6). The other two have gold documents that a reader would not pick for the question:
  *7 Essential Wix Tools* as the gold for "connect a custom domain" (rank 28) and *Index
  of Terms for SEO* for an AMP question (not in the top 100). These are synthetic
  questions generated *from* their gold article, so the label is where the question
  came from, not necessarily the best answer to it.
- Long questions retrieve better than short ones at every k (0.983 vs 0.966 at k=5),
  the same direction as on `dev`.
- Collapse ratio 1.081 mean, 1.20 p90 — the same shape as `dev` (1.102 / 1.20); zero
  exhaustion, so the pool of 50 is not binding (OQ-021 holds here too).
- Cost: $0.0023 for the run, $0.00000037 per query — exact, provider-reported.

## Correction (2026-09-16)

The Results section says every gold document is `article`. It is not: `dev_large` has
`article_type:article` n = 4,109, `article_type:feature_request` n = 2,049 and
`article_type:known_issue` n = 63 (from this run's own `slices_json`, surfaced by
EXP-0009's comparison). I had inspected only the `gold_docs` and `q_len` slices before
writing. Strict recall@5 for the control by type: article 0.976, feature_request 0.966,
known_issue 0.984. The `gold_docs:multi` statement stands. (MIS-022)

## Anomalies

p95 query latency 3,490 ms against a p50 of 325 ms: a long tail of slow DeepInfra
responses, one retry over 6,221 calls. Nothing failed. The run's first launch was on a
dirty tree and was killed (MIS-020).
