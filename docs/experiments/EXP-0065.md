## Question

Does reranking with TypeSafe Jev change strict recall@5 against the dense control
(OQ-053)? And how far apart are two identical Jev runs (OQ-055)?

## Configuration diff

Against `configs/promoted.yaml`:

```yaml
+reranker: typesafe_jev
+reranker_params:
+  model: jev-1.13.0
+  prompt_version: v1        # rerank_jev@v1: one noul (yes/no) question per chunk
+  workers: 8
+rerank_candidates: 50
```

Jev is asked once per candidate chunk whether the passage helps answer the question;
chunks are sorted by the returned probability. Same candidate shape as EXP-0024/25/26:
the top 50 distinct documents (11,680 chunk calls over 200 questions, 58.4 per question).

### A warning about the MDD labels

`rag compare`'s MDD block labels run 2's strict recall@5 Δ +0.025 **"significant"**.
That label is not valid for this run. It matches the run to the
`dense-control-v1` noise family (MDD ±0.012), which measures the query embeddings'
noise only. Jev adds its own: the two Jev runs differ by 0.015 on strict recall@5, more
than that MDD. The paired test (p = 0.598) is the decision. Logged as MIS-053.

## Observations

Read from the per-question rows: 29 questions were gained in **both** runs against the
control and 24 were lost in both. Eight of them, read in full:

- **Gained, `05364e00…`** ("Hoe can I restore the selection of projects for my Wix
  collection?"): dense ranked five Portfolio articles on the word "projects"; Jev put both
  gold CMS-restore articles at ranks 3 and 4.
- **Gained, `08bad226…`** ("do I have to give my login to a website designer"): dense
  ranked login-form articles; Jev ranked four collaborator-invite articles, gold among them.
- **Gained, `112b9450…`** (Wix Payments region/currency error): both gold articles moved
  to ranks 1 and 2.
- **Gained, `11c26d86…`** (URL not indexing on Google): the gold "Site or Pages Not
  Appearing in Search Engine Results" moved from outside the top 5 to rank 1.
- **Lost, `05cb6e04…`** (payouts on hold): one of the two gold articles was pushed out by
  "Understanding the Status of Your Account" (0.89) and "Updating Your Account Settings".
- **Lost, `06ed28b1…`** ("How to make the published changes draft?"): Jev ranked five
  Blog and Events articles about drafts and publishing above the gold Editor article.
- **Lost, `07e6923e…`** (free shipping for a sample option): the gold "Setting Up Free
  Shipping" fell out; Jev's top 5 included coupon and dropshipping articles.
- **Lost, `20be0f20…`** (promotion on an existing Core Plan): the gold "Using Wix
  Promotions for Premium Plan Renewals" was rank 1 under dense and left the top 5.

Top-5 probabilities sit close together (e.g. 0.89 / 0.88 / 0.88 / 0.87 / 0.87 for
`112b9450…`), at two-decimal resolution.

The `source:expertwritten` slice (n=100) shows +0.110 against the control in both runs
(p = 0.029 and 0.035), and `source:simulated` −0.090 / −0.060 (p = 0.19 / 0.39). This is
one slice of eight, tested twice; not a finding. Tracked as OQ-056.

## Anomalies

- **VOID first attempt** (`run_20260930_234257_a382`): an HTTP 520 from Cloudflare, not
  retried; usage recorded as 0 / $0 although calls were billed. MIS-052; fixed before
  these two runs.
- **Latency varied between identical runs:** p95 17,893 ms (run 1) vs 9,828 ms (run 2),
  9 retries in each. Per-call mean 189.5 ms vs 208.6 ms, so the p95 gap is in the tail.
- **Run 2 made 11,679 calls, run 1 11,680.** The candidate set comes from the dense
  retriever, whose query embeddings are not byte-deterministic (OQ-023); one question's
  50-document prefix had one fewer chunk.
- **Cost label.** Both rows say `cost_per_query_source: "exact: provider-reported …"`.
  For Jev it is reported tokens x the published rate. The runner now labels this
  correctly for later runs; these two rows keep the old label.
