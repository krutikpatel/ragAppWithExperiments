## Observations

- **The only candidate that improves ordinary articles on `dev_large`** (+0.007,
  p = 0.0025) as well as the `feature_request` set every candidate fixes. Its overall
  `dev_large` gain (+0.0153) is about half article-type effect and half not.
- **On `dev`, the direction agrees but the headline is not significant**: +0.030 is 6 net
  questions out of 200 (22 gained / 16 lost), CI [−0.030, +0.090], p = 0.41. The
  ranking metrics are: strict recall@1 +0.085 (p = 0.020), nDCG@10 +0.066 (p = 0.002),
  nDCG on single-document questions +0.080 (p = 0.0004). The gold document sits higher
  in the list more often; whether it is inside the top five moves less.
- **Multi-document questions: no measurable change on the strict metric** (+0.025, 7/6),
  but **every** multi-document question that had at least one gold in the top five still
  does, and four more now do (loose recall 0.850 → 0.950, 4 gained / 0 lost, p = 0.12).
  Multi-document nDCG is flat (+0.012).
- **Flipped `dev` questions are mostly large rank moves, not boundary shuffles**: gained
  *"extension on my payment date"* (gold 27 → 3), *"change my plan type from multi-year"*
  (9 → 4), *"header background to black when scrolling"* (8 → 3), and the two-gold *"last
  year's tax return for Wix Payments verification"* ([2, 6] → [1, 5]). Lost: two two-gold
  questions where the second gold slipped out (*"website automation… form submissions"*
  [2, 4] → [1, 8]; *"all available POS solutions in the UK"* [2, 5] → [2, 12]) and one
  one-place miss (*"animation to all images in a slider"* 5 → 7).
- **The promotion rule decides differently depending on which split decides.** DEC-050 as
  written (`dev_large` decides at p < 0.05, `dev` confirms direction): **passes**
  (`rag promote --dry-run`: both checks `passed: true`, no problems). The alternative
  put to Krutik under DEC-055 (`dev` decides at p < 0.05): **fails** on the headline
  metric, would pass on nDCG@10. EXP-0010 showed `dev_large` agreeing in sign with a
  16-point loss on `dev` is possible, so agreement in sign is weak confirmation.
- **Cost is the other half of the decision.** 20x the control's price per embedded token
  and 9x per query; on this corpus that is $0.63 per index build and $0.0000036 per
  query — negligible in absolute terms at 6,221 documents, and every chunking
  experiment in Axis 1 re-indexes at the promoted model's price ($0.63 × 5 runs vs
  $0.03 × 5).
- Lowest collapse ratio of the four models (1.049 / 1.036 mean); tightest latency tail.

## Anomalies

11 retries in total across 6,550 calls (2 during the build, 9 during `dev_large`
queries), all on transient statuses; no failures. `dev_large` actual cost was 9% under
the estimate.
