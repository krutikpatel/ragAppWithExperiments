## Question

What does the naive end-to-end system — dense retrieval, five distinct articles, one frozen
prompt, no citation enforcement, no refusal logic — score on the deterministic generation
metrics, and what do its failures look like?

## Observations

Written after reading the three refusals with gold in context, the two lowest-precision
answers with gold in context, two zero-step-coverage answers with gold in context, and two
"answered with gold missing" cases — nine questions in full.

**When retrieval succeeds the generator mostly cites correctly; when it fails the generator
answers anyway.** With every gold document in context, citation precision is 0.654 and recall
0.813. With a gold document missing, precision is 0.323, recall 0.192, and the generator
refused on **2 of 33** — it produced an answer for the other 31, grounded in whatever five
articles it was given. This is the same shape EXP-0001's Tier 2 found (58 of 60 answered on a
miss), on a retriever that misses far less often (33 of 100 instead of 60 of 100). The
no-refusal-logic baseline is doing what P1-07 run 3 measures directly.

**Three refusals with the gold article in context are the lexical detector working and the
generator hedging.** *"adjusting the page height … for a page without a footer"*: the gold
article *Changing the Length of Your Page* was in context and cited, and the answer opened
with "The provided articles do not cover this exact issue" before describing what the article
does say — a refusal by phrase, a partial answer by content. *"refund policy for canceling
my Premium Light account"*: the gold refund article was in context at rank 2; the model
refused because the articles do not name "Premium Light" specifically. *"my documents aren't
being accepted"*: the gold *Guidelines for Uploading Documentation* was in context; the model
refused because there are no "troubleshooting steps for rejected uploads". All three are
answerable from the retrieved text; the model asked for a closer match than the reference
answer needed.

**Citation precision loses to topically-adjacent articles, not to invented ids.** *"Right
Click Protect App"*: five right-click-protect articles retrieved, gold at rank 5, the model
cited the *Adding and Setting Up* article at rank 2 — precision 0 against a reference that
says almost the same thing. *"finding a Wix Partner"*: cited three of five retrieved articles,
none the gold *Hiring a Wix Professional* (rank 3). Not one of the nine answers read cited an
id that was not in its context.

**Step coverage of 0.289 is a mixture of matcher misses and real omissions, in unknown
proportion — the same caveat as EXP-0001, on a higher number.** *"set up all available POS
solutions in the UK"*: both gold articles in context, the answer is a numbered list keyed by
provider whose steps are nested under headings; the matcher finds none of the reference's
"Step 1 | Download the Wix app" steps. *"updating a PDF … dashboard CMS"*: gold in context and
cited, the answer is a correct-looking six-step sandbox-sync procedure, but the reference is
prose that describes syncing without steps; `n_reference_steps` was counted from a different
part of the reference. With gold in context, coverage is 0.320 (n=21); without, 0.229 (n=11).
OQ-007 still stands: this number describes the matcher as much as the answers.

**The format rule over-fires.** 80 of 100 answers are numbered lists; 32 of 100 references
are procedural. The prompt's "if the question asks how to do something" condition matched
most questions in this corpus, including *"I want to know more about the Right Click Protect
App"*. Step coverage is unaffected (it only scores procedural references), but any judged
correctness score on the other 48 is comparing a list to prose. Recorded for `v2`; the prompt
is frozen for this phase (DEC-042).

**Medium-length questions are the worst tercile on every metric here** (strict@5 0.458,
citation recall 0.465, step coverage 0.207) as they were in EXP-0001/0003/0005. Still no
established reason.

## Anomalies

- p95 latency 11,744 ms end to end: generator plus three judge criteria plus a hosted query
  embedding; zero retries, so this is the technique's latency, not a retry artefact.
- No judge failures, no generation retries, no URLs.
