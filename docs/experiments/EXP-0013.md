## Question

Quality versus dimension (P2-08): does asking the control for a quarter of its native
width change document-level retrieval? **Not** a cost question — see Observations.

## Observations

- **No measurable difference at a quarter of the width.** On `dev`, 11 of 200 questions
  changed outcome (4 up, 7 down; Δ −0.015, p = 0.55), nDCG@10 is tied on 133 of 200
  questions and moves by −0.001 overall, and the multi-document slice is unchanged
  (1 / 1). On `dev_large`, strict recall@5 is identical to four decimals; the only
  significant change anywhere is strict recall@1 on `dev_large`, −0.005 (p = 0.004) —
  half a point at the top rank on synthetic questions.
- **H-009 confirmed: width was not bge-m3's problem.** At the same 1024 dimensions the
  control scores 0.705 on `dev` where bge-m3 scored 0.560 (EXP-0010). The 3,072 discarded
  dimensions of the control were worth at most the one-point-of-two-hundred wobble
  above; bge-m3's 16-point deficit came with the model.
- **The flips are boundary shuffles, both ways**: gained *"customer's physical address on
  invoices"* (gold 9 → 5), *"reimbursement for a plan renewal"* (9 → 5); lost *"how do I
  get notified of purchases"* (4 → 11), *"animation to all images in a slider"* (5 → 8).
  Short questions lost most (−0.054, 4 / 0, p = 0.13), the same slice the prefix removal
  hurt most in EXP-0012.
- **Cost framing, as P2-08 requires:** the index is a quarter the size (33.7 vs 134.6 MB)
  and query-time cosine over 1024 floats is cheaper, but at 8,218 vectors neither is
  measurable here — the per-query latency difference is inside DeepInfra's jitter and
  the storage saved is 100 MB on a laptop. **The embedding bill is identical** (same
  tokens, same $0.0313). The cost argument for truncation only bites at a scale this
  project does not operate at — millions of vectors served under memory pressure —
  and this run says nothing about that scale.
