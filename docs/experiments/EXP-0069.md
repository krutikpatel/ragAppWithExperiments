with always sending 10 documents (EXP-0068)?

## Observations
Five questions read in full, in both runs:
- **`5c3ab38e…` — the empty context did NOT produce a refusal.** "How can I have the
  customer's physical address automatically appear on invoices…": the floor left no
  document, the prompt's ARTICLES section was empty, and gpt-5-nano wrote four generic
  steps ("Open your Wix dashboard and go to Settings…"), each cited `[doc:question]`, a
  document id that does not exist. The refusal detector did not flag it (refused = 0).
  DEC-102 assumed an empty context would be refused because the prompt says so; on the
  one case observed, it was not. FAILURES.md F23; OQ-061.
- **`9e730d7f…` (IRS letter of determination):** one document was left, and the answer
  was the fixed refusal naming what was missing. EXP-0068, with 10 documents and gold in
  context, answered with a hedge and cited a non-gold article (precision 0.0).
- **`525df6df…` (all POS options in the UK):** one document was left, the Square article.
  The answer cited only it (precision 0.5 → 1.0, recall 1.0 → 0.5) and named no SumUp
  setup steps. EXP-0068 gave per-provider steps.
- **`3da31b0c…` (pages not on the live site):** two documents were left. Precision
  0.33 → 1.0, recall unchanged at 0.5.
- **`275255f7…` (Error 403):** the gold article was still in the context (6 documents),
  but the answer refused, asking which scenario applied. EXP-0068 answered with steps
  citing the gold (precision 1.0). The floor did not remove the gold here; whether the
  smaller context or generator variation caused the refusal is not separable from one
  run.

## Anomalies

  - Stage latencies stored per question sum to about 14 minutes: retrieval 384.7 s, Jev
    139.2 s, generation 325.6 s.
  - The rest is not recorded by the runner.
  - Retrieval had a long tail: one query embedding took 83.2 s, the next 52.0 s.
    EXP-0068's maximum was 27.9 s.
- **A wrong diagnosis during the run.** At 06:03 Claude counted TypeSafe connections once
  a second, read about 129 Jev calls a minute, and told Krutik Jev was slow that day. The
  stored figures say otherwise: per-call mean 175.8 ms, against 177.5 ms in EXP-0068.
  Sampling once a second misses most 0.2-second connections.
- Jev's noise at Tier 2 has one paired measure here: EXP-0069's no-floor replay against
  EXP-0068, 1 question gained and 1 lost on gold in context.
