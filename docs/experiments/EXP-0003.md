## Question

P1-01 does not ask a retrieval question. It asks for the sparse baseline to be re-run under
the chunk config the dense control will use, so that the two controls differ only in the
retrieval method. The measurement that falls out — what moving BM25 from 512/0 to 600/100
did on this corpus — is recorded because it was measured, not because it was the goal.

## Configuration diff


## Observations

Written after reading all eleven questions whose strict recall@5 flipped (`rag diff
run_20260911_051054_ef08 run_20260912_213248_2471`).

**The aggregate did not move.** 6 questions gained strict recall@5, 5 lost it, 189 were
unchanged. The all / some / none split at k=5 is 82 / 19 / 99 against EXP-0001's 81 / 20 / 99:
the same 99 questions get nothing useful in the top five under either chunking. The failure
shape described in EXP-0001 — mostly total, worst on the second document of multi-document
questions — is unchanged by this config.

**Every flip is a small rank move across the k=5 line, and the gold articles involved are
mostly long.** Nine of the eleven gold documents that crossed the line are longer than 512
words, so they are chunked differently under the two configs. Gains: *Customizing Your
Horizontal Menu Design* (1,465 words) rank 16 → 3; *Receiving Your Payouts from Wix
Payments* (1,054) rank 9 → 2; *Requesting a Refund for a Premium or Studio Plan* (709) rank
11 → 4 and 8 → 5 on two different questions. Losses: *Customizing Automated Emails* (1,611)
rank 5 → 7; *Troubleshooting Wix Automations* (1,545) rank 5 → 6; *Canceling a Wix Premium
or Studio Plan* (874) rank 3 → 7. The two that are one chunk under both configs —
*Receiving Confirmation Emails When Orders Are Placed* (342 words, rank 8 → 5) and
*Purchasing Additional Business Emails* (141 words, rank 5 → 9) — did not change themselves;
the competitors around them did. Long articles moved in both directions; nothing in this
run says which way a given article will go.

**The slice moves are one to three questions each.** `source:expertwritten` fell by two
questions (0.410 → 0.390) and `source:simulated` rose by three (0.400 → 0.430); the
multi-document slice lost one question on loose recall (0.675 → 0.650) and none on strict.
These are the flips above, grouped, not slice-level effects.

**Chunk count fell 5.5%** (8,694 → 8,218) with 79.0% of articles now fitting in one chunk
(73.3% before; corpus profile, `EXPERIMENTS.md`). Four fifths of the corpus is chunked
identically under both configs — whole articles — which bounds how much any chunking change
can move on this corpus and is consistent with 189 of 200 questions not moving.

## Anomalies

None observed. `max_docs_returned` was 100 for every question; no k was capped by depth.
