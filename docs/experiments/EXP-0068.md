## Question
With Jev's ranking, does sending 10 documents to the generator instead of 5 change the
answers? EXP-0051 asked the same of the dense ranking (OQ-040).

## Configuration diff
```yaml
# vs configs/exp_0067_rerank_jev_tier2_dev.yaml
- top_k: 5
+ top_k: 10
```
`top_k` cannot change a retrieval metric (DEC-040/061); only generation metrics are
reported.

**OQ-058 answered: the same shape as OQ-040.** Ten documents put the gold article in
front of the generator for 14 more questions in 100, and citation precision fell

Against EXP-0051 (dense, k=10): gold in context 0.820 → 0.880 (p = 0.237), citation
precision −0.053 (p = 0.228), citation recall +0.032 (p = 0.561), cited nothing
0.080 → 0.010 (p = 0.016).

## Observations
Six questions read in full:
- **Precision fell, gold already in context** (`09e660b5…` lightbox background,
  `10bc9823…` Premium refund, `113cd9cf…` plan discounts): at k=5 each answer cited only
  the gold article (precision 1.00). At k=10 each still cited it and added one or two
  neighbouring articles (an ADI lightbox article, "Canceling a Wix Premium or Studio
  Plan", a Wix-app coupon article), precision 0.33–0.50.
- **Recall rose, gold newly in context** (`47ed1fdd…` sales reports, `5649c780…` Layers
  panel, `5ba30830…` booking calendar): the gold article was outside Jev's top 5 and the
  answer cited a neighbour; at k=10 it was in the prompt and the answer cited it.
- Against EXP-0051's split of the same kind, the "already" group lost less recall here
  (−0.014 against −0.075). One run each; not compared further.

## Anomalies
- Single run; Jev's noise at Tier 2 is unmeasured (OQ-055 measured it at Tier 1 only).
- p95 latency 17.6 s: the generator reads ×1.85 the input.
