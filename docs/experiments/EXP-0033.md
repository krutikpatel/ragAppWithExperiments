## Question
Do three paraphrases retrieved alongside the original and fused by RRF improve recall?

## Configuration diff
```yaml
+ query_transform: multi_query
+ query_transform_params: {model: openai/gpt-oss-20b, n: 3}
```
The original is kept — dropping it would discard the one phrasing known to be faithful.
**2.985 rewrites generated per question** (the parser dropped the occasional rewrite
that echoed the original verbatim), zero fallbacks.

**Delta exactly zero — 12 questions gained, 12 lost.** The cleanest null in the axis.

## Observations

**Zero overall, and a significant loss on short questions.** `q_len:short` fell 0.0946
at p = 0.016, with **7 questions lost and 0 gained** — the only slice in the axis where
one side is empty. A short question ("how to add more static pages to a Wix website")
is already close to a bare keyword query; three paraphrases of it add length and
synonyms that pull the fused ranking away from the literal match, and there is nothing
ambiguous for them to disambiguate. Medium-length questions moved the other way (+0.078,
p = 0.230), which is consistent but not significant.

**Paraphrases retrieve substantially different documents — 16.8 new ones per rewrite.**
This falsifies the reasoning behind H-025, which was that a meaning-preserving
paraphrase would retrieve nearly the same ranking and RRF would reproduce the original.
It is the same inversion EXP-0031 produced, at less than half the magnitude (16.8
against decomposition's 40.8), and the outcome tracks it: the technique that perturbs
the retrieval least is the one whose delta is exactly zero.

## Hypothesis
H-025: overall within ±0.02 — **correct**, and exactly 0.000. The mechanism was
**wrong**: I predicted `mean_new_docs_per_extra_query` **below 1.0** and measured
**16.8**. I reasoned that dense retrieval would be insensitive to phrasing; it is not.
I flagged that a high yield "would be a more interesting finding than the recall
number", and it is — this run and EXP-0031 together say the embedding space is far more
phrasing-sensitive than a "semantic search" framing suggests.

## Decision
**Control retained; not promoted.** Δ 0.000 at p = 1.000, at three extra embedding calls
per question.
