## Question
Can each candidate judge tell a supported answer from an unsupported one on this corpus,
well enough to gate CI? Pairs: a WixQA reference answer with its gold articles (labelled
*supported*), with random articles far from the question (*unsupported*), and with the dense
control's top non-gold articles (*hard negative*, report-only). Flag = faithfulness < 1.0.

## Observations
Read from the pairs, not only the aggregates.

- **Every judge flags every clearly unsupported pair.** Recall is 1.0 for all three, and
  the mean faithfulness on those pairs is 0.000–0.001. On the clear negative case the
  judges are not broken.
- **All three fail the same criterion by the same amount, and they agree on which answers.**
  On the 73 supported pairs all three scored, **58 got the same verdict from every judge**:
  36 flagged by all three, 22 passed by all three; 15 split. Judges from three different
  labs converging on the same 36 "supported" pairs is evidence about the pairs.
- **Five of the 36, read against their articles: the reference answers say things their
  gold articles do not.**
  - `acd87003` "how to handle multiple bookings for same registrant" — the answer describes
    *group bookings with a price per participant*; the gold article is about
    *multi-service appointments*, a different feature. Scores 0.00 / 0.00 / 0.00.
  - `9a57583e` "i cant see a visual representation of a page" — the answer is about the
    Layers panel and overlapping elements; the two gold articles are about images blocked
    by browser cache and mobile layout. 0.11 / 0.00 / 0.00.
  - `8bda79e2` "what is the Wix Studio Editor?" — the gold article is 49 words; the answer
    adds Studio's audience, design and dev-tool features from outside it. 0.25 / 0.10 / 0.00.
  - `9e730d7f` tax return for Wix Payments verification — the answer's "might not meet the
    requirements" and "typically a letter of determination is needed" are in neither
    article. 0.25 / 0.25 / 0.00.
  - `ecb61c75` sharing image — the answer's site-wide "General social image" steps go
    beyond the per-page settings the article describes. 0.09 / 0.00 / 0.00.
- **Simulated answers dominate the disagreement:** of the 36 flagged by all three, 21 are
  Simulated and 15 ExpertWritten; of the 22 passed by all three, 7 are Simulated and 15
  ExpertWritten.
- **So criterion 2 measured the labels, not the judges.** The "known supported" set assumed
  (Phase 3 fact 4) that every reference answer is grounded in its gold articles' text.
  For about half of this slice it is not: the answer carries support-expert knowledge,
  or the gold article is a near-neighbour of the right one. A judge that passed those
  pairs would be the lenient one.
- **Qwen's 22 failures are structured-output failures** — the model returned keys the
  schema did not ask for (`s` instead of `statement`) and four `instructor` attempts did
  not recover. 7 supported, 8 unsupported, 6 hard negative, plus the NaN.
- **Estimates were probe-calibrated on 2 questions and missed by −16% to +27%.** DeepSeek
  and Qwen billed 24% and 27% above the figures approved; Gemini 16% below. Total billed
  $3.69 against $3.89 approved.
