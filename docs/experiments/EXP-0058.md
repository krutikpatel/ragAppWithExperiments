## Question
Phase 3 fact 2: gold reached the generator for 0.67 of questions and 8 were refused, so
about a quarter were answered without the gold document. Are those answers grounded in
the non-gold chunks they did see, or invented?

## Observations
From a seeded sample of 30 of the 144 unsupported claims (seed 20260928), each read with
the judge's reason and labelled by hand. Categories emerged from the reading:

| Pattern | n / 30 | Example (question id, claim) |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Applies the article to the user's own case** — the claim names the user's specifics, which no help article can | 8 | `926d5b0f` "…submit the homepage URL of www.momsroots.com"; `07e6923e` "the sample still ships when the main product does not qualify" |
| **B. Conversational filler or navigation talk** | 8 | `6781a9f3` "The user is thanked for using automations."; `294aab11` "Related articles provide a general approach." |
| **C. Procedural drift** — a step paraphrased, implied or renamed | 7 | `7ca21618` "Open Wix Editor."; `f5ba9bc3` "In the Plan card settings, find the ribbon option." |
| **D. A factual error against the context** | 3 | `5c3ab38e` "Wix Events automatically adds custom fields to an invoice" — the article says it is **not possible**; `886b25a0`; `d3db98e7` |
| **E. Claim-splitting artifact** — Ragas's rewrite, not the answer's meaning | 4 | `a951bb42` "The website owner upgraded the site of the website owner." |

- **No answer in either group is wholly unsupported.** The answered-without-gold answers
  are mostly grounded in the non-gold chunks they saw: mean faithfulness 0.854, against
  0.866 with gold in context — a difference well inside its interval.
- **Partial support is the norm with or without gold**: 62% of gold-in-context answers
  also carry at least one unsupported claim.
- **Of the 30 unsupported claims read, 3 are factual errors.** 27 are the answer
  addressing the user, being polite, paraphrasing a step, or a decomposition artifact.
  The strict unsupported-answer rate therefore counts mostly style (OQ-048). It is
  unchanged here: it was declared before any data (P3-05).
