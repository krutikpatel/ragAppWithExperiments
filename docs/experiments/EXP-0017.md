  documents, 1% BM25-only — the mirror image of α=0.2.

  BM25-alone hits pulled up because dense had them at ranks 6–19 (*"add a full PDF to my
  portfolio site"* dense 9 / BM25 1 / fused 1; *"two different emails on my website"*
  19 / 2 / 4), and the rest are the "two mediocre ranks beat one" cases (*"cancel my
  Premium subscription within the 14-day period"* 6 / 7 / 4). These 13 questions are
  the whole of what lexical signal has to offer dense on this split, at any α.
- **The 16 losses are dense's rank 1–5 documents that BM25 missed** (8 absent from
  BM25's top 100, the rest at BM25 rank 17–88), pushed to fused rank 6–10 — just past
  the boundary: *"how to add more static pages"* (dense 1, BM25 absent, fused 6);
  *"Wix Partner to help redesign"* (3, absent, 6); *"how long does it take for my
  website to go live"* (1,5 / 36,absent / 1,10). At α=0.4 the same documents landed at
  rank 6–27; at 0.6 they sit at 6–10. The loss is shrinking with α but it is the same
  loss.
- **Multi-document slice: +0.050 (6 / 4, p = 0.75)** — the first hybrid point where it is
