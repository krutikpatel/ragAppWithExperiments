## Question
Phase 2 tested rerankers only at 50 candidate documents. The dense control has the gold
in its top 50 for 0.985 of questions, yet the best reranker put it in the top 5 for only
0.730. Two readings: the reranker is **drowning** in candidates (fewer would help), or it
is **not good enough at this task** (the count would not matter). OQ-038's rule: `rag
compare` on strict recall@5, ≥ 0.03 separation between the candidate counts.

over 200 questions. DEC-047 makes the paired test the decider for retrieval deltas, so
those labels are not used for the verdict.

## Observations
From the 15 questions whose outcome differs across the three candidate counts, read
individually with their gold ranks under each run:

- **Fewer candidates lost the questions dense search had buried.** All 4 questions that
  20 docs lost against 50 had their gold at dense rank **24, 27, 33** and a second gold
  at 18 — outside the 20-document window, so the reranker never saw them. At 50 docs it
  lifted each to rank 1 or 2 (e.g. `08bad226`, "do I have to give my login to a website
  designer": dense 33 → reranked 1).
- **More candidates pushed down gold that dense search had ranked well.** In the 8
  questions 20 docs won, dense search had gold at rank 1–6, and the gold's reranked rank
  worsened as candidates were added: `624105536`, "Hi I would like to install blog":
  **5 → 8 → 13** at 20 / 50 / 100 docs (dense had it at 1). `d327e4f6` (domain rename):
  5 → 6 → 9, dense 1. `96cf8f1e`: 4 → 7 → 8, dense 1. The extra documents are scored
  above the gold. This is the drowning signature, at the level of single questions.
- **The two effects nearly cancel.** 20 docs rescued 8 of these and lost 4; the net
  +0.020 against 50 docs is not significant.
- **Documents 51–100 rescued nobody.** 50 → 100 docs gained 0 questions and lost 3. With
  recall@50 at 0.985, at most 3 questions had gold available only past rank 50, and none
  of them was lifted into the top 5.
- **In 5 of these 15 questions the reranker moved dense's rank-1 gold down to 4 or
  worse**, at every candidate count. The reranker is not reliably better than dense
  search at the very top on these questions. That matches Axis 5's standing null.

## Anomalies
- **Cost estimate 2.1x low on EXP-0052** ($0.1881 estimated, $0.4001 billed). Cohere bills
  at least one search unit per query; the estimator scaled 1.17 units down linearly to
  0.47. Fixed in `rag/runner/cost.py` with a floor of one unit, tested. MIS-040.
- **EXP-0052's p95 latency (32,060 ms) is the query embedding, not the reranker.** Mean
  retrieval latency was 9,573 ms (max 98,699 ms), against 398 ms on EXP-0053 an hour later;
  mean rerank latency was 522 ms. It was a slow period at the embedding provider.
- **4 of 200 questions in EXP-0053 reached 87, 91, 92 and 97 candidate documents, not
  100** — 150 ranked chunks held fewer distinct documents for them. DEC-072's revisit
  trigger names this; at 2% of questions and 3–13 documents short, it does not change the
  reading.
- Rerank retries: 3 (EXP-0052) and 7 (EXP-0053), all recovered; no rate limits.
- The first launch of this pair failed before `rag` started (a log redirect into
  `temp/`, which is a file). No row, no spend. MIS-041.
