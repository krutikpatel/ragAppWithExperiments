## Question

Does dense retrieval beat lexical retrieval on this corpus, with everything else held at the
reconciled control config? The Phase 1 handover framed BM25 as "a serious opponent here, not
a straw man" because the corpus is dense with exact product names.

## Configuration diff

```yaml

-  k1: 1.5
-  b: 0.75
+retriever: dense
+retriever_params:
+  embedding_backend: openrouter
```

## Observations

Written after reading the nine questions dense lost and a random six of the seventy it gained
(`rag diff run_20260912_215235_ff2f run_20260912_222538_b1dc`).

**Dense beat lexical on this corpus, on every slice, by a margin far outside either run's
spread.** 70 questions gained strict recall@5, 9 lost, 121 unchanged. The all / some / none
split at k=5 is **143 / 20 / 37** against BM25's 82 / 19 / 99: the number of questions that
get nothing useful in the top five fell from 99 to 37. The handover's expectation that BM25
would be a serious opponent on this product-name-heavy corpus was not borne out at the
document level; whether it holds on specific exact-term questions is not something this
run's slices can separate.

**The gap is largest where BM25 was worst.** Single-document questions went 0.469 → 0.806.
Multi-document strict recall doubled, 0.175 → 0.350, but remains the weakest slice by far:
on the 40 multi-gold questions, loose recall@5 is 0.850 — dense finds *one* of the required
articles for 34 of 40 — while all of them reach the top five for 14. The second-document
problem EXP-0001 identified is halved, not solved. Medium-length questions (13–17 words)
remain the worst length tercile under both retrievers (0.344 → 0.562); the reason is still
not established.

**Misses are now almost entirely ranking misses.** 64 gold documents are outside the dense
top-5; 62 of them are inside the top-100 (31 at ranks 6–10, 16 at 11–20, 15 at 21–100) and
**2** are not, against BM25's 136 missing with 49 outside the top-100. The vocabulary
failures EXP-0001 described — *published* vs *visibility*, *static pages* — have largely
become rank-6-to-10 failures.

**What the gains look like.** *"i want to change my plan"* (five words, no product term):
gold *Changing Your Premium or Studio Plan* was not in BM25's top-100 and is dense rank 2.
*"how long does it take for my website to go live"*: *About Domain Propagation* moved from
rank 36 to rank 1. *"allow users to enter the amount they want to pay"*: the gold *Adding
Payments to a Site Form* moved from rank 88 to 1. These are questions whose wording shares
no distinctive token with the gold article's title.

**What the losses look like.** All nine are one-to-two-word exact matches that BM25 rewarded
and dense ranked lower: *"add a full PDF to my portfolio site"* — *PDF Viewer App* rank 1 →
9; *"header background to black when scrolling"* — *Adding Header Scroll Effects* rank 1 →
8; *"extension on my payment date"* — *Changing the Payment Date for Recurring
Subscriptions* rank 3 → 27; *"give my login to a website designer"* — *Roles & Permissions:
Inviting People to Collaborate* rank 2 → 33. Three of the nine are the same refund /
auto-renewal pair of articles, on three differently worded refund questions, dropping from
ranks 1–5 to 5–14. This is the lexical-match case the handover anticipated; it exists, and it
is nine questions against seventy.

**Collapse is no worse under dense.** Mean ratio 1.106 vs 1.088; p90 1.40 vs 1.20; deepest
walk 11 chunks (2.2). The pool of 50 was never approached (OQ-021). A model that clustered
an article's chunks together would show here; this one does not, on this corpus.

## Anomalies

- **The canonical run's p95 latency (2,441 ms) is inflated by retries**: 7 of its 329 calls
  were retried after transport errors (recorded in `retriever_meta`). The replicates, with
  zero retries, show p95 of 635 and 665 ms; p50 is 289–325 ms across all three. Report the
  replicate p95 as the latency of the technique.
- **Hosted query embeddings are not deterministic** (see Spread). It moved no aggregate by
  more than one question here, but it means a dense run has a floor of ~0.005 on
  recall metrics that BM25 does not, and `rag diff` between two dense runs of the same
  config will show a handful of flips. Recorded as OQ-023.
- The first index-build attempt was VOID on an unretried `httpx.ReadError` (MIS-014).
