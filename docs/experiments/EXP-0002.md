## Question

P0-14: does the WixQA paper's own retrieval setup (Cohen et al., arXiv:2505.08643 —
BM25, whole documents, top-5) score differently from our chunked baseline on labelled
metrics? And, separately: what does the paper's configuration score on *our* metrics,
so the narrative has an anchor to the published setup?

This is **not a reproduction of the paper's numbers**. The paper reports an LLM-judged
Context Recall, not labelled recall; its BM25 implementation and tokenizer are not
stated. See DEC-036.

## Configuration diff

```yaml
chunker_params:
-  chunk_size: 512
+  chunk_size: 7000     # longest article is 6,653 words -> every document is one chunk
```

One axis. Everything else identical to EXP-0001.

## Observations

**No measurable difference on the headline.** +0.005 strict recall@5 is one question
net out of 200: `rag diff` shows **11 questions gained and 10 lost**, 179 unchanged.
Run-to-run spread is zero for both configs, so this is not noise in the usual sense —
it is two configurations that disagree on 21 questions in opposite directions and
cancel. That is "no measurable difference", and it is written as such.

**Deeper recall is slightly lower with whole documents.** strict recall@20 drops
0.635 → 0.610. Ten questions whose gold documents sat at ranks 6-20 under chunking
sit lower under whole documents. Not investigated further here.

**Latency drops 35 → 21 ms p95**, consistent with scoring 6,221 items instead of 8,694.
Irrelevant at this scale.

**The flips show no length story.** The gold documents of gained questions have median
length 716 words; of lost questions, 660. Chunking can only change the text of the
1,661 documents over 512 words (27% of the corpus), but it also changes BM25's
document-length normalisation for every document, which is presumably why short
documents move too. The rank shifts on the eight flips read in full are small in both
directions (e.g. 7→3, 9→3 gained; 3→6, 5→8 lost).

**The multi-gold slice moves 0.175 → 0.200** — one question of 40. Not a finding.

**What this says about the paper's setup.** On labelled strict recall@5, BM25 over
whole documents finds all required articles for 41% of `dev` questions. The paper's
Context Recall for BM25 on ExpertWritten is 0.72-0.73, but that is a GPT-4o judgment
of whether the *information needed* is present in the top-5, on all 200 ExpertWritten
questions rather than our 100+100 dev split. The two numbers measure different things
and are not placed side by side (DEC-036).

## Anomalies

None observed. Every document became exactly one chunk (6,221 chunks for 6,221
documents), confirming `chunk_size: 7000` is "no chunking".
