## Question
How small a regression can the gate actually catch — per judged metric at golden-slice
size, and for recall@5 under the paired test at α = 0.05?

## Observations
- **The judge is most of the judged noise.** Holding the answers fixed and re-judging moved
  mean faithfulness by 0.023, against 0.026 for fully fresh runs; unsupported-answer rate
  0.057 against 0.064.
- **Refusals move only when answers are regenerated**: 4, 9 and 9 of 80 answerable
  questions refused across the three runs; identical under re-judging. 11, 10 and 10 of 15
  unanswerable questions refused.
- **Citation integrity varies with no change at all**: 1 of 79, 2 of 76 and 1 of 76 answers
  cite an id not in their context. A zero-tolerance gate rule would fail every run.
- **One answer contained a URL-shaped string** (`47337149…`, run 1): `www.mystunningwebsite.com`,
  Wix's example domain quoted verbatim from an article in its context (36 articles use
  it). First occurrence in any stored `baseline_answer@v1` answer; recorded as a reviewed
  exception in `tests/test_prompts.py` (DEC-083). The test still fails on anything new.
